## C1 (Advanced)

1. **Specialized Professional Terminology**: Industry-specific jargon, technical precision in various fields
2. **Theoretical Frameworks & Models**: Conceptual models, theoretical constructs, paradigm shifts
3. **Complex Linguistic Structures**: Sophisticated syntax, register variation, discourse markers
4. **Cultural Anthropology & Sociology**: Kinship systems, social stratification, cultural relativism
5. **Advanced Scientific Methodology**: Research design, statistical analysis, peer review processes
6. **Philosophical Traditions & Schools**: Existentialism, phenomenology, analytical philosophy
7. **Geopolitical Analysis**: Power dynamics, strategic alliances, diplomatic protocols
8. **Organizational Psychology**: Leadership theories, team dynamics, change management
9. **Advanced Literary Theory**: Postmodernism, narratology, hermeneutics
10. **Cognitive Science & Neuroscience**: Neural networks, consciousness studies, cognitive biases
11. **Advanced Linguistic Theory**: Morphology, phonology, syntax, semantics
12. **Macroeconomic Theory**: Fiscal policy, monetary systems, economic modeling
13. **Legal Philosophy & Jurisprudence**: Natural law, legal positivism, constitutional interpretation
14. **Advanced Medical Sciences**: Pathophysiology, pharmacology, clinical research
15. **Computational Sciences**: Algorithms, data structures, artificial intelligence principles
16. **Advanced Environmental Science**: Ecosystem dynamics, biodiversity conservation, climate modeling
17. **Advanced Historical Analysis**: Historiography, primary source analysis, historical methodology
18. **Interdisciplinary Studies**: Cross-field applications, synthetic approaches, boundary crossing
19. **Advanced Communication Theory**: Rhetoric, persuasion, intercultural communication
20. **Advanced Mathematics & Statistics**: Probability theory, inferential statistics, mathematical modeling

## C2 (Proficiency)

1. **Nuanced Expressions & Idiomatic Language**: Subtleties of meaning, extensive range of idioms, phrasal verbs
2. **Abstract Concepts & Metaphors**: Philosophical abstraction, conceptual frameworks, metaphorical language
3. **Specialized Terminology (Cross-Disciplinary)**: Vocabulary across various academic and professional fields
4. **Complex Relationships & Interdependencies**: Analyzing intricate systems, cause-effect chains
5. **Sophisticated Argumentation & Rhetoric**: Advanced persuasive techniques, logical fallacies, debate skills
6. **Epistemological Discourse**: Theories of knowledge, truth, justification, skepticism
7. **Advanced Aesthetic Theory**: Beauty, sublime, artistic interpretation, critical aesthetics
8. **Metacognitive Processes**: Thinking about thinking, self-regulation, metalinguistic awareness
9. **Phenomenological Description**: Consciousness, lived experience, intentionality
10. **Hermeneutical Analysis**: Interpretation theory, meaning-making, textual analysis
11. **Dialectical Reasoning**: Thesis-antithesis-synthesis, contradictions, resolution
12. **Semiotic Analysis**: Signs, symbols, meaning systems, cultural codes
13. **Discourse Analysis**: Power relations in language, ideology, social construction
14. **Pragmatic Competence**: Speech acts, conversational implicature, context-dependent meaning
15. **Stylistic Variation**: Register, tone, voice, literary devices
16. **Intertextuality**: Text relationships, allusion, pastiche, parody
17. **Deconstructive Reading**: Questioning assumptions, undermining binaries, revealing tensions
18. **Comparative Analysis**: Cross-cultural comparison, contrastive studies, synthesis
19. **Theoretical Synthesis**: Integrating multiple perspectives, creating new frameworks
20. **Precision in Specialized Domains**: Exact terminology in highly specific fields (quantum mechanics, constitutional law, etc.)
