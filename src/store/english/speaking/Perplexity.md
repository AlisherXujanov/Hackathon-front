# Professional Speaking Practice for IELTS English Learning

To add a professional-speaking practice section to your IELTS English learning website without a budget for expensive AI models, you have several effective and cost-efficient options. Many modern solutions leverage free or freemium AI-based tools specifically designed for language learners, making it possible to deliver high-quality speaking practice to your users.

## Recommended Free & Low-Cost Approaches

### 1. Integrate Free AI-Powered Speaking Platforms

Several platforms offer AI-based speaking practice (including IELTS-style exercises) with free usage tiers. These tools provide instant feedback, pronunciation correction, and conversational prompts:

**Talkpal**: Offers IELTS-focused speaking simulations, instant AI feedback, interactive dialogues, and peer community features. Its free plan includes detailed feedback and realistic IELTS test practice, making it especially relevant for your needs.

**ELSA Speak**: Specializes in pronunciation and accent training using AI-powered speech recognition. ELSA's free version offers substantial pronunciation exercises and scoring.

**Gliglish**: Focused on building speaking fluency and confidence, Gliglish uses AI-driven role play and scenario-based conversations. It offers feedback on pronunciation and grammar in a friendly, motivating environment.

**SmallTalk2Me**: Includes an IELTS Speaking Test Simulator with instant band scoring, automated fluency analysis, and daily speaking activities—available in its free plan.

Most of these platforms embed easily via links or can be referenced for your users. Some offer API access or embeddable widgets, allowing you to integrate them into your site for seamless user experience.

### 2. Use Public Speech Recognition APIs for DIY Practice (Non-AI Approach)

If you want to avoid third-party AI completely, consider integrating public speech recognition services (like the free browser-based Speech Recognition API). This allows users to:

- Record their answers to modeled IELTS speaking prompts.
- Compare their transcripts to model answers.
- Self-assess using rubrics or peer feedback.

This method does not provide rich AI feedback but is affordable, customizable, and can help users become comfortable with speaking.

### 3. Structured Human Interaction and Peer Review

Leverage your community by enabling:

- **User-to-user speaking practice**: Set up a speaking partner or group session feature using video or voice calls (even via external platforms like Zoom, Google Meet, or integrated open-source video chat solutions).
- **Peer recording & feedback exchange**: Let users upload or record their answers for others to review against IELTS criteria, fostering collaborative improvement.

### 4. Utilize Open Educational Resources

Supplement your speaking section with:

- Scripted speaking drills and mock IELTS questions.
- Recording exercises with detailed self-assessment or peer review checklists.
- Curated YouTube or podcast activities focused on IELTS speaking.

## How to Implement

**Easiest**: Provide external links to free AI tools such as Talkpal, Gliglish, or ELSA Speak. Encourage users to practice there as part of your "Speaking" curriculum.

**Best for Website Integration**: Use open speech-recognition APIs and guide users through answering IELTS prompts, then offer text feedback or allow peer ratings.

**Hybrid**: Combine AI tools (for instant, automated feedback) with a peer community for additional support, motivation, and practice.

## What to Avoid

- Relying on costly AI APIs, as most (like OpenAI's advanced models) are not truly free for production-scale use.
- Requiring teacher review for every practice unless you have volunteers or a scalable peer-to-peer system.

---

By leveraging high-quality, free tools like Talkpal, ELSA Speak, or Gliglish, together with structured speaking activities and peer practice, you can offer a robust, professional, and cost-effective speaking practice experience for your IELTS platform users—without major investment.
